The game data is NOT included.

- "The HUNT Begins" (the shareware episode, HUNTBGIN.WAD, .RTL, .RTC,
  REMOTE1.RTS) is Apogee's. Its VENDOR.DOC lets anyone pass the shareware
  around free of charge, but only as the whole, unmodified DOS package (all
  its files, ROTT.EXE and the rest) "on IBM compatible media"; an Amiga
  drawer holding only the data files is not that. So the port does not ship
  it: get the shareware archive (1rott13.zip, ROTTSW13.SHR inside it, which
  is a ZIP archive) and copy its data files into the drawer.
- The registered game (DARKWAR.WAD and the rest) is sold by Apogee
  (3D Realms), GOG and Steam; bring your own copy.
